Privacy Policy

Tiny Disciples  |  Last updated: June 5, 2026

Tiny Disciples is designed for children. We are committed to protecting the privacy of children and complying with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and Google Play's Families Policy.

1. About This App

Tiny Disciples ("the App") is a Bible-based educational app for children. It is developed and published by Digital Evangelist (jackson.isbd@gmail.com).

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information from users. The App does not require account registration, a name, email address, phone number, or any other identifying information.

The only data stored by the App is:

  • App progress & preferences – your child's selected avatar and completed activities are saved locally on the device using the device's built-in storage. This data never leaves the device and is never transmitted to us or any third party.

3. Third-Party Analytics

The App does not include any third-party analytics SDK. We do not collect or transmit usage statistics, crash reports, or behavioral data.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App displays ads served by Google AdMob. Because this is a children's app, AdMob is configured as follows:

  • Child-directed treatment is enabled (tagForChildDirectedTreatment = true).
  • Under-age-of-consent flag is enabled (tagForUnderAgeOfConsent = true).
  • Maximum ad content rating is set to G (family-safe content only).
  • Non-personalized ads are requested (nonPersonalizedAds = true). No behavioral profiling or interest-based targeting is performed.

Google may collect certain non-personally identifiable, device-level information (such as device model and OS version) solely for the purpose of delivering contextual, non-personalized ads. This is governed by Google's own policies:

  • Google AdMob Families Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, rent, or otherwise share any user data with third parties, except as described in Section 4 (AdMob ad delivery). No data is shared with data brokers, marketing platforms, or any other external services.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

This App is directed to children under the age of 13. In compliance with COPPA:

  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
  • We do not allow children to share personal information through the App.
  • Ads are served with child-directed treatment and non-personalized settings as described in Section 4.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at jackson.isbd@gmail.com and we will promptly delete it.

7. Permissions

The App requests no sensitive device permissions. It does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, location, or any files on your device.

The App requires an internet connection solely to load advertisements via AdMob.

8. Data Security

All data saved by the App (progress and preferences) is stored only on your device using standard platform storage mechanisms. Since we do not transmit this data, it is not subject to network-level risks.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage parents and guardians to review this policy periodically. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
